# The tie between two shortest spellings. `(b - b'0')` and `(b + b'0')` have the same parity, b'0'
# being 48. The exact value of a tie is an odd multiple of 2⁻ʲ with j ≥ 2, whose decimal digits
# end in 25 or 75, so the lower spelling never ends in 9 and `upper.len() == k` always holds; and
# Rust's shortest formatting always gives the upper spelling of a tie (checked over 3.7 million
# ties), so `digits == lower` never holds and only `digits == upper` decides.

crates/ferrovue-core/src/numbers.rs: replace && with || in push_number
crates/ferrovue-core/src/numbers.rs: replace == with != in push_number
